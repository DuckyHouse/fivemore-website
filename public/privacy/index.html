Privacy Policy

Who We Are

This privacy policy applies to FiveMore, which is operated by Developer. It covers the app on iOS and watchOS.

What Data We Collect

Information users provide directly may include:

  • Account and sign-in details needed to create or manage a user account
  • Files, documents, or user-generated content handled through the app
  • Health or fitness data used by health-related features
  • Purchase or subscription information used to unlock or manage paid features

Information collected automatically through the app or related services may include:

  • Usage and interaction data about how the app or related services are used

Additional data sent off the device may include:

  • workout analytics

Some data leaves the device to support app functionality such as accounts, syncing, purchases, support, or other online features.

Some of this data may be linked to a person, account, or persistent identifier.

How We Use Data

We use your data in a variety of ways, including:

  • To understand product usage and improve the app.
  • To personalize the app experience for the user.
  • To manage billing-related access and entitlements.
  • To provide the AI feature the user chose to use.

The following providers are used:

  • Payments: RevenueCat. Used for subscriptions and receipt handling.

Additional feature details:

  • HealthKit: Reads basic data available in the Health App.

When Data is Shared

When an app feature needs online processing, syncing, account handling, or support, relevant data may be sent off the device to operate that feature.

Some data is shared with outside service providers that help operate the app or related services.

The following providers are used:

  • Payments: RevenueCat. Used for subscriptions and receipt handling. Optional for users.

Payments and Subscriptions

The app offers in-app purchases or subscriptions through StoreKit.

Payment-related providers include RevenueCat. They are used for subscriptions and receipt handling.

Purchase or subscription information may be used to manage billing, entitlements, support, refunds, or account access.

Data Retention

We keep data until the user deletes it, closes the account, or asks us to remove it, unless a longer retention period is legally required.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data, but no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices and Rights

Some permissions are optional and are only used if the user chooses the feature that needs them.

Users can ask for deletion of their data using the contact or tools described below.

Where supported, users can ask for a copy or export of their data.

Where supported, users can request account closure or account-related deletion.

Privacy requests can be sent through ruan@duckyhouse.dev.

Requests can be handled through In-app deletion tools and In-app export tools.

Children’s Privacy

This app is not directed to children. Parents can contact us by email.

AI Features

The app includes AI-powered features. When a user chooses to use those features, prompts, uploads, or related content may be sent to an AI provider to return a result.

AI-related providers include Apple Intellefence FoundationModels, OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, and Grok.

These providers are used for they are used to interpret transcribed text into workout models to use inside the app.

AI usage is optional and only happens if the user chooses the AI feature.

AI-related inputs or outputs may be retained according to the retention approach described in this policy.
